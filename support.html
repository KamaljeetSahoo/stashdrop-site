Support

Getting set up, and what to do when something doesn't connect.

What you need

  • A Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
  • An iPhone running iOS 26 or later.
  • Both on the same Wi-Fi network — or your Mac joined to your iPhone's Personal Hotspot.

Getting started

  1. Download Stashdrop for Mac, open the disk image, and drag Stashdrop into Applications.
  2. Open it. It lives in your menu bar. If it asks to find devices on your local network, choose Allow.
  3. From the menu bar icon, choose Pair New iPhone. A code appears.
  4. Open Stashdrop on your iPhone, tap Scan the QR code, and point the camera at your Mac. Both screens will confirm once they've connected.

If something isn't working

My iPhone can't find my Mac
  • Check both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, and that your Mac is awake with Stashdrop running in the menu bar.
  • On your iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network — make sure Stashdrop is on.
  • On your Mac: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network — make sure Stashdrop is on. If it was off, quit and reopen Stashdrop.
  • Café, hotel and office Wi-Fi often stop devices seeing each other. Turn on your iPhone's Personal Hotspot and join it from your Mac instead — it works anywhere.
  • Tap the connection pill at the top of the iPhone app to search again.
“Your Mac's identity changed”

This appears when your Mac presents a different certificate than the one your iPhone paired with. It's expected if you reinstalled Stashdrop on your Mac or set up a new Mac — pair again. If you didn't do either of those, don't pair: something else may be pretending to be your Mac.

Only some of my photos show up

You've given Stashdrop access to selected photos only. Tap Select More in the banner, or allow full access in Settings › Stashdrop › Photos.

Where do my files go on the Mac?

Into a folder called Stashdrop in your home folder, sorted by year and month. They're ordinary files — open them in Finder, or use Move to… in the Stashdrop window to put a batch onto an external drive.

Is it safe to delete photos from my iPhone?

Only once they show the green check in the photo grid — that means every byte arrived and matched. Stashdrop never deletes anything on its own. If you use iCloud Photos, remember that deleting on your iPhone also deletes from iCloud.

A transfer stopped halfway

Nothing is lost. A file only counts once it has fully arrived and its checksum matches; anything partial is discarded. Send it again — files that already arrived are skipped, and nothing is ever duplicated.

Unpairing a device

On your iPhone, open Settings › Unpair This Mac. Re-pairing the same iPhone later replaces its old entry on your Mac rather than adding another.
